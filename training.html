Develop mental health support skills responsibly.

Foundational learners may study mental health awareness, active listening, psychological first aid, ethics, safeguarding, suicide risk recognition and referral. Qualified counselling professionals can pursue further learning appropriate to their existing credentials.

PROFESSIONAL

Counsellor Development

For appropriately qualified practitioners. Care360 Plus independently checks credentials, experience, applicable registration and scope of practice before considering counselling-service access.

Training, certification and provider access are separate decisions

Training or a certificate may strengthen an application but never guarantees approval to Care360 Plus. A short course does not make a person a licensed nurse, doctor or independent mental health counsellor. Only describe certificates as accredited or internationally recognised after confirming the issuer and recognition. Live course availability and fees must be verified with the training provider.
