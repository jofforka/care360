Development roadmap

A transparent view of what comes next.

Current: Beta

Early-access Android installs, invitation-based iOS testing, app demonstrations once published, direct private feedback and product evaluation.

Before wider rollout

Usability, security, privacy, clinical-safety and operational readiness validation; controlled partner evaluations where approved.

Proposed: Version 2

Additional functionality including AI-assisted capabilities is planned, not activated. Timing, scope and release depend on responsible assessment and technical readiness.
