How it works

Four steps to join testing

1

Select your app

Choose Care360 Plus or Care360 Patient.

2

Select your phone

Install from Google Play on Android, or request TestFlight access for iPhone.

3

Get access

Android: install from Google Play. iPhone: share your email privately with our team for TestFlight invitation.

4

Share feedback

Report bugs and usability issues without including private health information.

Testing and privacy notice

Android builds are available on Google Play; iOS participation remains invitation-based through TestFlight. Never send personal medical records, identity documents or identifiable patient data through general WhatsApp feedback. Use demo data until a separately authorised secure clinical pilot is approved. Training certificates do not guarantee Care360 Plus provider approval.
